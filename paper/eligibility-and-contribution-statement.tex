\section*{3. Use of AI Assistance}

The lead author used Claude (Anthropic), including the Claude Code programming assistant, during development and writing: for drafting and refactoring code under the author's direction, for generating figures from the committed evaluation files, and for polishing the language of the manuscript. The assistant's involvement is recorded transparently in the repository: commits it contributed to carry a \texttt{Co-Authored-By} trailer. All design decisions, data-handling rules, experiments, verification, and the claims made in the report are the authors' own and were reviewed by the authors, who take full responsibility for the code and the text.
