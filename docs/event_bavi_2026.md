# Event dossier: Super Typhoon Bavi (2026 · 2609)

Compiled 2026-07-10/11 (pre-landfall). Every fact carries a source; forecast
statements are marked as forecasts and will be reconciled against best-track
data post-event.

## Identity

- International number **2609** (WP092026), name **Bavi**（巴威, contributed
  by Vietnam — the Ba Vì massif near Hanoi）; PAGASA name **Inday**.
- Ninth named storm of the 2026 Pacific typhoon season.

Peak intensity on record so far: **910 hPa**; JTWC one-minute winds
285 km/h (155 kt); JMA ten-minute winds 205 km/h; CMA 225 km/h (62 m/s).

## Track and size

- Overall motion: northwest at ~22 km/h; 2026-07-11 03:00 JST position
  23°42′N 125°18′E (south of Miyako-jima).
- Reported circulation diameter ~**1,300 km** (state media), one of the
  largest systems of the season.
- Affected during transit: Marshall Islands, Marianas, **Philippines**
  (Mindanao rainbands), Ryukyu Islands, Taiwan, East China.

## Forecast landfall (as of compilation)

- CMA forecast: landfall **night of 2026-07-11 CST** on the
  **Xiapu (Fujian) – Wenling (Zhejiang)** coastal segment.
- Forecast hazards (2026-07-10 to 07-13): heavy to torrential rain in
  southern Zhejiang and northeastern Fujian, locally extreme; sustained
  Beaufort-10 coastal winds for ~20 hours in central-northern Fujian
  (Ningde and Fuzhou county-level high wind-hazard risk); red (highest)
  nearshore wave warning; high seawater-backflow inundation risk for
  low-lying coastal zones of southern Zhejiang and northern Fujian on the
  night of 07-11.
- Secondary hazards flagged by CMA: small-river floods, flash floods,
  urban/rural waterlogging, landslides.

## Casualties and impact (pre-China-landfall)

- Philippines: **≥18 dead, 8 injured, 5 missing** — Malapatan, Sarangani
  (10 dead); Calanogas, Lanao del Sur (6 dead, 5 missing); Bukidnon (2 dead).
